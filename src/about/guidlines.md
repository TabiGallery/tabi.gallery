# Tabi Community Guidelines

For more information, our full code of conduct and official policies are available HERE \[insert link\]

### HAVE FUN

Make friends, make art, play games, watch movies and enjoy yourself

### BE KIND

Don’t punch down. Avoid hateful language, jokes or rhetoric.

### BE SOBER

Tabi in a drug and alcohol free space

### HELP YOURSELF TO UNLABELLED FOOD IN FRIDGE

Feel free to use anything in the fridge that isn’t labeled with a name or initial

### CLEAN UP AFTER YOURSELF

Do your dishes and leave the space as-good-or-better-than you found it

### BE MINDFUL OF OTHERS IN THE SPACE

Tabi should stay a chill space for those with sensory needs. Avoid rambunctious behaviour that would disrupt that.

### LISTEN TO MARGINALIZED VOICES

When necessary: apologise, modify your behaviour and move on without getting defensive or demanding an impromptu debate.

### RESPECT THE PRIVACY OF THE PRIVATE STUDIOS

Our Tabi Studio Artists keep us afloat, pls don’t piss them off

##### Reach out to a Tabling (either in person or via Discord or Mastodon) if you have any accessibility needs, questions or concerns
